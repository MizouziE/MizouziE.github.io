# Emotional Intelligence

A tough, but worthwhile read. Laden with anecdotal and academic citation evidence, the discovery of the importance of emotions in a person's development and well-being is presented and meticulously explained from the biology and neuroscience though to the anthropologic and social science. How our day to day interactions are influenced by and can have an influence on our brain's inner workings has never made so much sense.
